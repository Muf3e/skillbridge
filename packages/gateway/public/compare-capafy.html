The Fundamental Flaw in Traditional Skill Marketplaces

When platforms distribute skills as local downloadable packages (`SKILL.md` or scripts), buyers inevitably reverse-engineer the prompts after a single purchase. Creators end up doing unpaid research for users who cancel immediately.

The SkillBridge Zero-Leak Paradigm

SkillBridge acts as an authenticated remote gateway. The buyer's AI agent in Claude Code, Cursor, or terminal CLIs issues standard tool calls, but the actual execution happens inside hardened micro-containers. The buyer receives the clean structured output, while the creator's prompts and proprietary logic never leave our servers.
